Cookie Policy

Cookies and similar browser storage help websites remember essential settings and deliver secure account access. BrightSum will use only essential storage until optional analytics are added.

Essential storage

Essential storage may be used for secure sign-in, payment/session security, saving cookie choices and temporary learning features. This storage is required for the service to function.

Optional analytics

BrightSum will not set non-essential analytics or marketing cookies unless you actively consent. If analytics are introduced, this page and a cookie settings control will list the provider, purpose and storage period. You will be able to reject or withdraw optional-cookie consent at any time.

Local prototype data

The current local BrightSum prototype may save demonstration learning questions in your browser’s local storage. This stays on the device until it is cleared. The published service will explain all account and learning-data storage in its Privacy Policy.
